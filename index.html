THE PROBLEM

They read how you move.

Cameras and radar on every street measure your breathing, your heartbeat and the way you walk. They don't need your face to know it's you.

01 · HEADGEAR

One thing on your head.

A horned helmet with a field coil inside. The field flows down over your whole body, so signals hit the field, not you.

01 · HEADGEAR · INSIDE

Mirror, then field.

The metal shell bounces the signal away. Under it, a coil sends out a field of its own, so nothing reaches your head.

02 · SHRUG

Chains, a shiny blanket and a black electronics bag, worn as a short cape. The same field, from a different place.

03 · BRACELET

Or just your wrist.

The smallest thing still covers all of you. A foil cuff, loose links, and a coil that never stops talking.
