04

Do's and don'ts.

Read this before you wear any of it.

DO

  • Keep a soft layer between metal and bare skin.
  • Leave gaps so air can move. Foil and plastic get hot.
  • Check you can still see, hear and turn your head.
  • Tape down sharp ends of wire, forks and caps.
  • Take it off if you feel dizzy, hot or short of breath.
  • Share what worked. Copy it, change it, pass it on.

DON'T

  • Cover your nose or mouth with foil or plastic.
  • Wrap wire tight around your neck, wrist or ankle.
  • Wear it while driving, cycling or near traffic.
  • Go near open flames, heaters or power lines.
  • Plug the coil into the wall. Small batteries only.
  • Let it stop you from getting help in an emergency.
