UNREAD

A blueprint for not being read. Copy it. Share it. Make it.

SCROLL ↓
01 · THE PROBLEM

They read how you move.

Cameras and radar on every street measure your breathing, your heartbeat and the way you walk. They don't need your face to know it's you.

02 · THE ANSWER

Get dressed.

Each piece covers one thing the machine reads. Watch them come together.

03 · COVERED

Cover what gives you away.

Your face, your throat, your chest, your hands, the way you walk. Each piece hides one of them. Switch outfits at the top to compare.

04 · REFLECT

Metal is a mirror.

To a radar, metal works like a mirror. The signal hits the crown and bounces off in the wrong direction. Nothing useful comes back.

05 · SCATTER

Make noise.

Hundreds of loose metal strands move on their own. Each one sends back its own tiny movement, and your real movement gets lost in the crowd.

06 · ABSORB

Swallow the rest.

Zoom into the coat wall. Some signal slips past the metal. The carbon layer behind it soaks it up before it reaches your skin.

07 · HOLD STILL INSIDE

Breathe in private.

A stiff layer holds the coat away from your chest. You breathe and your heart beats, but the outside surface doesn't move.

08 · WALK

A shape, not a person.

A street robot looks right at it. It knows something is there. It can't tell who.
